## D-1 — Norman's Affordance Law

**Source:** `docs/laws/sources/B1-norman/laws.md`

## D-2 — Gulf of Execution / Evaluation

**Source:** `docs/laws/sources/B1-norman/laws.md`

## D-3 — Perception Bias Law

**Source:** `docs/laws/sources/B9-johnson/laws.md`

**Source:** `docs/laws/sources/B9-johnson/laws.md`

## D-6 — Peripheral Vision Law

**Source:** `docs/laws/sources/B9-johnson/laws.md`

## D-7 — Inattentional Blindness

**Source:** `docs/laws/sources/B5-weinschenk/laws.md`

## D-9 — Recognition Over Recall

**Source:** `docs/laws/sources/B9-johnson/laws.md`
