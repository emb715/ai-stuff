## D-8 — Miller's Constraint


**Fleet summary:** Working memory holds approximately 4 items simultaneously (Weinschenk's empirical update of Miller's original 7±2).


**Source:** Thing 20 — People Remember Only Four Items at Once (raw lines 2309-2380)
**Key quote:** "For example, when you provide links that people can go to for more information, limit the number oflinks to three or four. lf you can’t limit the number of links, topics, or choices to three or four, chunk or group information into three or four groups."
