## D-13 — PARC Principle

**Fleet summary:** Four forces govern all visual composition: Proximity, Alignment, Repetition, Contrast. Nothing on the surface is arbitrary.


**Source chapter:** Ch 1 — *Proximity* (raw lines 534-1303)
**Key quote:** "When several items are in close proximity to each other, they become one visual unit rather than several separate units."



**Excerpt from chapter:**

> When several items are in close proximity to each other, they become one
>      visual unit rather than several separate units. Items relating to each other
>      should be grouped together. Be conscious of where your eye is going: where do
>      you start looking; what path do you follow; where do you end up; after you've
>      read it, where does your eye go next? You should be able to follow a logical
>      progression through the piece, from a definite beginning to a definite end.

## D-26 — Trust-Through-Consistency

**Fleet summary:** Repetition of patterns teaches users the system's rules. Each time a pattern is violated, trust is fractured.


**Source chapter:** Ch 3 — *Repetition* (raw lines 2200-2865)
