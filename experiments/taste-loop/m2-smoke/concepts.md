# Tidewell concepts

## C1 Tide table (risk)
- Idea: coffee arrives on a rhythm, like a tide; the page reads as a tide chart so the fortnight cycle is the brand.
- Preset: premium. Type: Familjen Grotesk display + JetBrains Mono data. Palette: deep sea-navy ground, amber (crema) accent used sparingly, cool.
- Skeleton: left-aligned hero with a full-width tide curve; "how it works" as a tide-table of rows; plans as a ruled table with one outlined column.
- Signature: a 28-day tide curve in the hero whose crests are the deliveries.

## C2 Roast tag (bold)
- Idea: a loud, warm, poster-like shelf label; confident and playful.
- Preset: playful. Type: Bricolage Grotesque display + DM Sans. Palette: saturated tomato-orange ground, ink type, one pale mint block.
- Skeleton: split hero, giant type left, stamped hang-tag right; big numbered steps; three tall cards in different fills.
- Signature: an oversized hang-tag with origin, altitude, roast date.

## C3 Provenance ledger (safe, on-brief)
- Idea: trust through traceability; every bag has a farm, a roaster, a date.
- Preset: trustworthy. Type: Fraunces display + Public Sans. Palette: pale sage light, forest green accent.
- Skeleton: left editorial hero with ledger card; three numbered columns; plans with a tinted band.
- Signature: a "this fortnight's bag" ledger entry as the hero object.
